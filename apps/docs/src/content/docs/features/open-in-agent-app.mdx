{productName} is built to orchestrate agents across your projects and sessions. It doesn't try to be the best place to work with a single agent: Claude, Codex and similar apps are excellent at that, with powerful features and a rich experience, and {productName} doesn't want to duplicate them. When you feel the next task needs a more capable app, you can continue the session's work in that agent's own app. The conversation carries on there, so nothing is redone.

<Screenshot id="open-in-app-menu" alt="An interrupted session's menu with “Open in Claude app” after “Hand off”, and the note that the agent's app may not see this conversation." />

## Why it works this way

- <strong>{productName} orchestrates, the agent's app is for working in depth.</strong> Keeping each to its job means you get both: many agents and projects in one place, and the full app when one task deserves it.
- <strong>It's a bridge, not a copy.</strong> {productName} only asks the app to show the session's conversation: nothing is copied, the session isn't changed and nothing it runs is stopped. The session stays interrupted or archived in {productName}; if you resume or reopen it there later, the app shouldn't be writing to it at the same time.
- <strong>Only a stopped session can be opened.</strong> The app and {productName} would both write to the conversation while the agent runs in {productName}, so the action is disabled while the session has a process, with <strong>Session running</strong> under it. Stop the session first, for example by archiving it. A session with no conversation yet has nothing to open: <strong>No conversation yet</strong>.
- <strong>It happens on the daemon's computer.</strong> For a remote window, the app opens on the remote machine, not on the computer the window runs on. The action is offered only on a Mac or Windows PC, and only when the agent's app is installed there. Grok Build has no app, so it never shows the action.
- <strong>The app reads the agent's default directory only.</strong> If the conversation's latest record lives in an account's own directory (an account that doesn't share the default configuration), the item carries a note, <strong>The agent's app may not see this conversation</strong>. It only warns: the action still works, but the app may show an older state, or nothing.

## How to use it

<Steps>

1. Stop the session if it's running: open its menu and choose <strong>Archive</strong>.
2. Open the session's menu and choose <strong>Open in Claude app</strong> or <strong>Open in Codex app</strong>, depending on its agent. On an interrupted session it sits right after <strong>Hand off</strong>; on an archived one, right after <strong>Reopen</strong>. Its shortcut is <strong>W</strong>.
3. Or open <strong>View archive</strong> from the project's menu, choose <strong>View all</strong>, hover the session's row and press the window icon. It's the first of the row's buttons, before <strong>Reopen</strong>.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="open-in-app-archive" alt="The archive view with a row's three buttons shown on hover: “Open in the agent's app” first (the window icon), then “Reopen” and “Delete”." />

You know it worked when the app comes forward with the conversation. If it doesn't, an alert titled <strong>Could not open the agent's app</strong> says the app may not be installed, may not have started, or has no conversation to open. Its details give the cause, and you can try again.

When the app takes over the conversation, any wait {productName} was holding to bring the session back on its own, such as a wait for an account's usage limit to reset, ends.
