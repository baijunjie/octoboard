{productName} is built to orchestrate agents across your projects and sessions. It doesn't try to be the best place to work with a single agent: Claude, Codex and similar apps are excellent at that, with powerful features and a rich experience, and {productName} doesn't want to duplicate them. When you feel the next task needs a more capable app, you can continue the session's work in that agent's own app. The conversation carries on there, so nothing is redone.

<Screenshot id="open-in-app-menu" alt="An interrupted session's menu with “Open in Claude app” after “Hand off”, and the note that the agent's app may not see this conversation." />

## Why it works this way

- <strong>{productName} orchestrates, the agent's app is for working in depth.</strong> Keeping each to its job means you get both: many agents and projects in one place, and the full app when one task deserves it.
- <strong>It's a bridge, not a copy.</strong> {productName} only asks the app to show the session's conversation: nothing is copied.
- <strong>The session is archived first, like a handed-off one.</strong> The app and {productName} must not both write to the conversation, and an interrupted session would come back as soon as you select it. So a session that isn't archived yet is archived before the app opens, together with the sessions under it, after a confirmation. Its branches and worktrees are kept, since the app works in the session's folder, and you choose which of its background processes keep running. Reopen it from the archive once you're done in the app, to continue here.
- <strong>A session at work can't be opened.</strong> While the session is working or waiting for you, the action is disabled with <strong>Session busy</strong> under it; wait until it's waiting for instructions. A locked session shows <strong>Session locked</strong>, and a session with no conversation yet has nothing to open: <strong>No conversation yet</strong>.
- <strong>It happens on the daemon's computer.</strong> For a remote window, the app opens on the remote machine, not on the computer the window runs on. The action is offered only on a Mac or Windows PC, and only when the agent's app is installed there. Grok Build has no app, so it never shows the action.
- <strong>The app reads the agent's default directory only.</strong> If the conversation's latest record lives in an account's own directory (an account that doesn't share the default configuration), the item carries a note, <strong>The agent's app may not see this conversation</strong>. It only warns: the action still works, but the app may show an older state, or nothing.

## How to use it

<Steps>

1. Open the session's menu and choose <strong>Open in Claude app</strong> or <strong>Open in Codex app</strong>, depending on its agent. On an interrupted session it sits right after <strong>Hand off</strong>; on an archived one, right after <strong>Reopen</strong>. Its shortcut is <strong>W</strong>.
2. If the session isn't archived yet, confirm. The confirmation lists the sessions archived with it and its background processes: uncheck any process that should keep running.
3. Or, for an archived session, open <strong>View archive</strong> from the project's menu, choose <strong>View all</strong>, hover the session's row and press the window icon. It's the first of the row's buttons, before <strong>Reopen</strong>.

</Steps>

<Screenshot id="open-in-app-archive" alt="The archive view with a row's three buttons shown on hover: “Open in the agent's app” first (the window icon), then “Reopen” and “Delete”." />

You know it worked when the app comes forward with the conversation. If it doesn't, an alert titled <strong>Could not open the agent's app</strong> says the app may not be installed, may not have started, or has no conversation to open. Its details give the cause, and you can try again. If the session's process doesn't stop in time, the app isn't opened and the session stays archived: wait a moment, then try again.

Any wait {productName} was holding to bring the session back on its own, such as a wait for an account's usage limit to reset, ends when the session is archived or the app takes over.
